# Returns and refunds

## Return window
You can return any unused item within **30 days of delivery**. Items must be clean and in their original packaging. Worn base layers and opened socks can only be returned if they are faulty.

## How refunds are paid
Refunds go back to the original payment method. After we receive the return, the refund is issued within **2 business days**, and banks usually show it within **5 to 7 business days**.

## Damaged or faulty items
If an item arrives damaged, contact support within **14 days of delivery** with a photo. We offer a replacement or a refund, and you do not need to send the item back if it is worth less than $30.

## Partial refunds
Support staff can issue partial refunds for minor faults (for example a scratch or a missing strap). Refunds above $50 need a manager's approval.

## Cancelled orders
Orders can be cancelled free of charge until they ship. Once an order has shipped it cannot be cancelled; return it instead.
