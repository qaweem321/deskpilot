# Warranty

## What is covered
All Northpeak gear has a **2-year warranty** against manufacturing defects, counted from the delivery date. Backpacks and trekking poles have a **lifetime warranty** on frames and buckles.

## What is not covered
Normal wear and tear, damage from accidents, and repairs done by someone else are not covered.

## How to claim
Open a support ticket with your order number and photos of the defect. We repair the item, replace it, or refund it if neither is possible.
