# Shipping

## Costs
Standard shipping costs **$6.95**, and it is **free on orders over $75**. Express shipping costs **$14.95**.

## Delivery times
- Standard: 3 to 5 business days.
- Express: 1 to 2 business days, if ordered before 2 pm UTC.
Orders are packed within one business day.

## Tracking
You get a tracking link by email when the parcel ships. If tracking has not changed for **5 business days**, contact support and we will open an investigation with the carrier.

## Countries
We ship to the United States, Canada, the United Kingdom and the European Union. Customs fees outside the EU are paid by the customer.
