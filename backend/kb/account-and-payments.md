# Account and payments

## Payment methods
We accept Visa, Mastercard, American Express, PayPal and Apple Pay. All prices are in US dollars and include VAT for EU orders.

## Changing your email address
Support can change the email address on your account after confirming the request from your current email address.

## Invoices
Invoices are emailed when the order ships and can be downloaded from the account page.

## Deleting your account
Ask support to delete your account. Order records are kept for 7 years for tax reasons, without your contact details.
